Student Assessment with Python (synthetic sample)
Use at most 400 words in your written explanations.

Class marks (out of 100):
Name | Maths | Physics | English
Asha | 78 | 82 | 91
Ben | 55 | 61 | 70
Chen | 92 | 88 | 84
Dara | 40 | 47 | 58
Eli | 67 | 73 | 65

1. Compute the average of Asha's three marks: (78 + 82 + 91) / 3.
2. Write a Python function letter_grade(mark) that returns "A" for 80 and above, "B" for 65 to 79, "C" for 50 to 64 and "F" below 50.
3. Write a Python function class_average(marks) that takes a list of numbers and returns their mean.
4. Explain in two or three sentences why the average alone can hide weak students in a class.
